# Merit: product concept v1

**Merit, the Talent Intelligence Engine.** Status: concept locked for the first presentation. Audience: executive and
people leaders at global (Silicon Valley-grade) companies.

> **One line:** Merit follows every person from first résumé to fifth year, turns scattered work signals into a fair,
> explainable picture of contribution, and decides nothing without a human.

Category: **Evidence-Based Performance**. Existing tools manage forms; Merit manages evidence.

## 1. Seven evidence layers

| # | Layer | Sources | Nature |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Psychometric core | MBTI, CliftonStrengths, DISC (self-insight); Big Five/HEXACO, work-style (validated, for decisions) | Who you are |
| 02 | Pre-hire | Résumé, candidate-shared LinkedIn, structured interview, work sample | The promise |
| 03 | Intent | OKRs, project management, commitments | The plan |
| 04 | Proof of work | Git, tickets, ERP, docs: outcomes, not activity | The execution |
| 05 | Collaboration | Slack, Teams, email patterns: who unblocks whom (consented, metadata-first) | The invisible work |
| 06 | Employee voice | Weekly voice check-in and check-out on WhatsApp, Teams or Slack | Their story |
| 07 | Manager & peer voice | Monthly Tap & Speak, lightweight peer recognition | Others' view |

## 2. The engine: Claims → Evidence → Context

Chosen over a weighted score (unfair, gameable, not explainable), a black-box AI rating (what the EU AI Act and NYC
Local Law 144 target), and plain three-way triangulation (absorbed).

1. **Claims:** everything said about a person (résumé, interview, self voice, manager voice, peer kudos) is a claim.
2. **Evidence:** the engine links each claim to support, contradiction or "no data" in layers 03–05, with a receipt.
3. **Context:** before judging the person it checks the system: dependencies, scope changes, on-call and incident load.
4. **Portrait:** the output is an evidence profile with confidence. No master score, never a verdict.

Hook: *Every performance tool grades the person. Merit grades the evidence and checks the system first.*

## 3. Algorithms around the engine

| Algorithm | Job |
|---|---|
| Signal-to-Success | Which pre-hire signals actually predicted success in this company and role; recalibrates interviewers. |
| Contribution Graph | Network analysis of who unblocks whom; powers the Silent Hero Detector. |
| Growth Trajectory | Skill-growth slope, time to productivity, promotion readiness. |
| Fairness Guard | Audits the evaluators: recency, halo, similarity and proximity bias; rating skew; adverse impact. |
| Team Chemistry | Strengths plus real collaboration patterns to suggest balanced project teams. |
| Retention Radar | Early overload and flight-risk signals from workload patterns (never emotions); triggers a stay conversation. |

## 4. Outputs

Candidate Evidence Dossier · 30/60/90 Ramp · 1:1 Card before every one-on-one · Tap & Speak (a 12-person team in
about seven minutes, with a Blind Spot card) · Drafted, evidence-backed review both sides can see and contest ·
Staffing Engine · My Mirror and the Working-With-Me Card for every employee · voice questions for leadership.

## 5. Trust rules

1. No decision without a human. AI drafts; people decide and explain.
2. Every judgment has receipts the person can see.
3. No emotion or tone scoring; no keystroke or screen surveillance. (The EU AI Act prohibits workplace emotion recognition.)
4. Missing evidence is not poor performance.
5. Psychometrics describe; they never decide. (MBTI and CliftonStrengths publishers say not to use them for selection.)
6. The evaluators are audited too.
7. Employees own their profile; collaboration analysis is opt-in and aggregated.

## 6. Visual identity

- **Symbol: the Merit Portrait.** A solid silhouette (the person) inside seven growth rings (the layers). Each band
  is as thick as its evidence; missing evidence is dotted. Every person's portrait is unique.
- **Mood:** warm paper `#F4F1EA`, ink `#16140F`, calm pen-plotter motion. Deliberately the opposite of the dark
  Revenue Orchestrator deck.
- **Type:** Instrument Serif (display), Inter (UI), IBM Plex Mono (data).
- **Layer colors:** 01 `#C4553B` · 02 `#D49A3A` · 03 `#8C9A52` · 04 `#2E9384` · 05 `#3F6DB0` · 06 `#7558A3` · 07 `#B0507A`.
- **Logo:** silhouette with one-color ink rings. The seven colors are for data, never the logo.

## 7. Positioning

Review/survey tools (Lattice, Culture Amp, 15Five) manage forms. Talent-matching AI (Eightfold, Workday) stops at the
hire. Interview AI (HireVue) is a black box. Collaboration analytics (Viva Insights, Worklytics) risk surveillance.
Engineering metrics (Jellyfish, LinearB) count activity in one function. Merit: one evidence trail from first résumé
to fifth year, a context engine that separates person from system, and an algorithm that audits the evaluators.
